Mimoza is a private photo album for your family and close friends. You make a circle, invite the people who belong in it, and everyone in the circle shares one feed and one album. Nobody else can see it.

Post a photo with a caption, comment on what other people post, and react with emoji. Each circle has its own album, grouped by month, so an old photo is easy to find again.

END TO END ENCRYPTED
Photos, captions, comments and reactions are encrypted on your phone with keys that only the members of the circle have. Mimoza stores the encrypted content and cannot read it.

INVITE ONLY
A circle is the people you invited. To add someone, send them an invite link and approve their request to join. There are no public profiles and no way to find a circle you were not invited to, so a family album stays within the family.

LESS TO COLLECT
Mimoza keeps your name and, if you add one, a profile picture, so the people you invite can see who is asking. It does not store your email address. Your information is not sold and not used for ads.

YOUR ACCOUNT FOLLOWS YOU
Sign in with Apple or Google on a new phone and your circles come with you, keys included. You can also scan a code from your old phone to move over directly. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time, which also removes the photos, comments and reactions you posted.

You choose which circles notify you. Mimoza is available in English, Turkish, Spanish, French and German.
