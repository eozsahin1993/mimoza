Mimoza is a private place for the photos you would only show your favorite people.

Make a circle for your family, your closest friends, or the group chat that never stops. Everyone in it shares one feed. Post a photo, write a caption, leave a comment, react with as many emoji as the moment deserves. Every circle keeps its own album, in the order you lived it.

Nobody outside the circle can see any of it. Not the public, not advertisers, not us.

END TO END ENCRYPTED, FOR REAL
Photos, captions, comments and reactions are encrypted on your phone with keys that only the members of a circle hold. Mimoza stores that content but cannot read it. The same goes for your profile picture.

LESS TO COLLECT, LESS TO WORRY ABOUT
The only profile details Mimoza keeps are your name and, if you add one, a picture. No email address is ever stored. Your information is never sold and never used for advertising.

SMALL ON PURPOSE
No follower counts, no algorithm, no strangers. A circle is the people you invited and nobody else. Invite with a link, and they are in.

YOUR ACCOUNT FOLLOWS YOU
Sign in with Apple or Google on a new phone and your circles come with you, keys included. Delete your account from inside the app whenever you like, and everything you shared goes with it.

Mimoza is for the photos that matter to a few people, not the whole internet.
