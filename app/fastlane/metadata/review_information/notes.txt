Sign-in is with Apple or Google only, so there is no demo account to provide. Any Apple ID or Google account works, including a brand new one.

Everything is reachable from one fresh account with no invite and no second device:
1. Sign in with Apple, enter a name.
2. Tap the plus button to create a circle.
3. Open the circle and post a photo from the photo library, with a caption.
4. Tap the photo to comment or react.

Invites and joining: the circle's details screen (top right of the feed) has an invite link. Opening it on another signed-in account joins that circle after the admin approves the request.

Content is end to end encrypted. Photos, captions, comments and reactions are encrypted on the device with keys held only by the circle's members; our servers store the ciphertext and cannot read it.

Reporting objectionable content: open any photo someone else posted, tap the menu (top right), and choose "Report this photo". It is also in Settings, "Send feedback", "Report a post". Both are sent from inside the app; no mail app is needed. A report identifies the photo and its circle, which is enough for us to find the entry and remove it.

Blocking: a circle admin can remove any member, which also rotates the circle's keys so the removed member can read nothing new. Anyone can leave a circle at any time.

Account deletion is in Settings and erases the account and everything it shared, server side.
